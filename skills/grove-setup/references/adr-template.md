# ADR template (Matt Pocock convention)

Copy this into `docs/adr/NNNN-kebab-title.md` when `grove-design` or the human
drafts a new ADR. Number sequentially from the highest existing ADR + 1.

```markdown
# NNNN. Title of the decision

Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](./NNNN-other.md)

## Context

What forces (technical, product, team) led to this decision being needed.

## Decision

What we decided, stated as a clear, declarative sentence.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include the rejected alternatives
and why they lost, briefly.
```
